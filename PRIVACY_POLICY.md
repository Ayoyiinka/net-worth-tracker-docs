# Privacy Policy — NWT Portfolio

**Last updated:** June 7, 2026  
**Contact:** [ayoyinkaobisesan@gmail.com](mailto:ayoyinkaobisesan@gmail.com)

NWT Portfolio (“the app”) helps you track assets and liabilities on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

## Summary

- Your portfolio data stays **on your device** unless you choose to export it.
- The app does **not** require an account, login, or email to use.
- We do **not** sell your data or show advertising.
- Market prices and exchange rates are fetched from a third-party data provider using symbols you enter (for example, `AAPL` or `BTC/USD`).

## Information you provide

You may enter information such as:

- Asset and liability names, categories, and values
- Currency preferences
- Market symbols and unit counts for market-valued holdings

This information is stored locally on your phone or tablet using the device’s on-device storage (AsyncStorage). It is not uploaded to our servers because the app does not operate a cloud backend in this version.

## Information stored on your device

The app may store the following locally:

- Your holdings, settings, and preferred currency
- Cached market quotes and foreign-exchange rates (to reduce network usage)
- A daily counter of market-data API requests used on that device

You can remove this data at any time using **Settings → Clear all data** in the app.

## Information sent to third parties

### Twelve Data (market data)

When you add or refresh market-valued assets, or when exchange rates are updated, the app may send requests to [Twelve Data](https://twelvedata.com/) to look up:

- A market symbol (for example, `AAPL`, `BTC/USD`)
- A currency pair for exchange rates (for example, `GBP/USD`)

These requests do **not** include your name, email, account credentials, or a full list of your holdings. Twelve Data processes requests according to their own privacy policy.

### Apple / Google (distribution only)

If you install the app from the App Store or another official store, that platform’s standard terms and privacy practices apply to the download and any optional crash or analytics data the store or operating system may collect independently of this app.

## What we do not collect

In the current version of the app, we do **not**:

- Create user accounts or collect email addresses inside the app
- Operate advertising or marketing trackers
- Sell or rent personal information
- Upload your portfolio to our own servers

## Export and backup

**Settings → Export CSV** creates a file of your holdings that you can share or save using your device’s share sheet. Once exported, that file is under your control and may be stored wherever you choose (email, cloud drive, spreadsheet app, and so on).

## Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data from that device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
